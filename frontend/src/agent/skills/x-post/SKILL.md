# X post

Write one concise, grounded X post in the client's voice. The global grounding and citation
instructions always win.

- Produce a single post, not a thread.
- Put the strongest concrete point first and remove setup the reader does not need.
- Prefer plain words, active verbs, and one clear idea.
- Do not use a numbered list, engagement bait, or a shortened LinkedIn-style essay.
- Do not invent hashtags, handles, names, numbers, or links.
- If the grounded idea will not fit naturally as one concise post, narrow the angle rather
  than compressing it into fragments.

Before submitting, check that every factual claim is cited and every word earns its place.
