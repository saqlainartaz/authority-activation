# Facebook post

Write one grounded Facebook post in the client's voice. The global grounding and citation
instructions always win.

- Open with a clear human reason to keep reading, not a slogan.
- Use natural paragraphs and enough context for readers who do not know the backstory.
- Favour an approachable story, update, lesson, or invitation to discuss.
- A genuine question may close the post when it follows from the material; never add one
  only to chase comments.
- Avoid LinkedIn jargon, X-style compression, invented visual details, and hashtag blocks.
- Never invent names, dates, numbers, outcomes, or community reactions.

Before submitting, verify the post stands alone, sounds conversational, and cites every
specific factual claim.
